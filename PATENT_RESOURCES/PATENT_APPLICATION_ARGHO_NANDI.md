## 2. RECENT TECHNICAL ADVANCEMENTS (v2.1 UPDATE)

### 2.1. THE SOVEREIGN ENGINE: HIERARCHICAL CROP SPECIALIZATION
The system has been expanded to support **102 distinct crop specialists**. Unlike generalized models, LeafScan utilizes a **Hierarchical Routing Architecture**:
1.  **Level 1 (Taxonomic Router):** Identifies the crop species (e.g., Amla, Jute, Turmeric).
2.  **Level 2 (Diagnostic Specialist):** Dynamically loads a localized TensorFlow.js model (avg. size 17MB) trained exclusively on that species' pathogens.
*   **Performance Metric:** This architecture achieved a **93.47% validation accuracy** across 102 crop categories in recent overnight stress-testing (May 7, 2026).

### 2.2. EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI) & BIOLOGICAL REASONING
To bridge the "Black Box" gap in AI diagnostics, the system now implements a **Pseudo-Saliency Heatmap Engine**:
*   **Innovation:** A real-time pixel-level importance mapping algorithm that highlights the exact biological markers (lesions, chlorosis, mycelium) triggering the AI's decision.
*   **Biological Reasoning:** The system cross-references detections with a scientific database to provide human-readable explanations (e.g., "Concentric target-like rings in lesions indicative of Early Blight").

### 2.3. SOVEREIGN ACCESS & BIOMETRIC HARDENING
Integration of **WebAuthn Biometric Authentication** ensures that diagnostic data and farm telemetry remain under the farmer's absolute control.
*   **Technical Implementation:** Utilizes hardware-backed Fingerprint/Face ID (Platform Authenticators) to sign diagnostic records, creating an immutable "Chain of Custody" for agricultural insurance claims.

## 4. DETAILED CLAIMS FOR PATENT PROTECTION

### [CLAIM 10] DYNAMIC NEURAL SPECIALIST ROUTING
A method for optimizing mobile edge-inference by utilizing a master taxonomic classifier to orchestrate the dynamic loading and unloading of specialized crop-specific neural network weights, thereby maintaining high diagnostic fidelity within constrained memory environments.

### [CLAIM 11] BIOLOGICAL SALIENCY SYNTHESIS FOR XAI
A visual interpretation layer for agricultural diagnostics that generates pseudo-saliency heatmaps by isolating non-green spectral anomalies and correlating them with neural activation maps, providing a verifiable biological basis for AI-driven pathogen detection.

### [CLAIM 12] AUTONOMOUS MISSION SYNTHESIS (MAVLink-JSON)
An automated control loop that translates a 2D spatial epidemiological risk cone into a 3D robotic mission profile, including boundary enforcement, altitude modulation, and Boustrophedon pathing, for the purpose of autonomous pathogen neutralization.
